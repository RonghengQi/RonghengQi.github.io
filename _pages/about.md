I am an early-career researcher working at the intersection of GeoAI, remote sensing, urban analytics, and disaster risk. My work focuses on using multi-source geospatial data and machine learning methods to better understand environmental hazards and urban systems.

My current interests include flood susceptibility assessment, Earth observation data fusion, interpretable machine learning, and spatial decision support for resilient cities.
