My research focuses on GeoAI, remote sensing, and disaster risk. I combine multi-source geospatial datasets, spatial analysis, and machine learning to study environmental hazards and urban systems.

Research Themes
======

### Theme 1: Flood Susceptibility and Disaster Risk

I study spatial patterns of flood susceptibility and disaster exposure using geospatial predictors, statistical learning, and interpretable machine learning.

### Theme 2: GeoAI and Remote Sensing

I work with Earth observation, GIS, and urban spatial datasets to extract useful information for environmental monitoring and planning.

### Theme 3: Urban Analytics

I am interested in how spatial data, urban morphology, and data-driven modelling can support resilient and sustainable cities.

Current Projects
======

- **Flood susceptibility modelling**: Developing spatial machine learning workflows for flood-prone area identification.
- **Multi-source urban remote sensing**: Exploring how Earth observation and urban datasets can describe built-environment patterns.

Methods and Tools
======

- Python, R, and geospatial data processing
- GIS, remote sensing, and spatial statistics
- Machine learning, model interpretation, and reproducible analysis
